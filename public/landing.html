The problem

Trust breaks when one fraud story goes viral.

A smart contract cannot know if a story is true. CareFund does not pretend to. It limits what a false story is able to do.

Typical crowdfunding

  • Money lands in the requester's own account
  • Anyone can post any story
  • Donors hope the money is used well
  • Nobody is accountable for a lie

With CareFund

  • Grants are paid to the hospital, pharmacy or provider
  • A registered verifier must approve every case
  • Money is released in milestones, against a bill
  • Goal missed, cancelled or frozen: donors get a refund
How it works

Four steps from request to receipt.

1

A person asks for help

They pick a grant or a loan and a verifier they trust. Only a fingerprint of their documents goes on-chain; the files stay private.

2

The verifier checks and names the payee

A hospital, doctor or NGO confirms the documents and sets the wallet that gets paid. For a loan, they also lock a 20% guarantee.

3

Donors or lenders fund it

The money waits inside the contract. If the goal is not reached in time, every donor is refunded automatically.

4

Released in milestones, with proof

Each part is released only when the verifier submits the fingerprint of a bill. Loans are repaid in parts and lenders claim their share.

Safeguards

Nine rules the contract enforces itself.

✔

Registered verifiers

Only verifiers approved by the owner can open a case for donations.

🏥

Money to the provider

A grant can never be paid to the requester's own wallet.

🧾

Milestones with proof

Funds stay in escrow and are released in parts, each against a bill hash.

↺

Automatic refunds

Goal missed, cancelled or frozen: donors take back what has not been released.

⚑

Reports from real donors

Only people who gave can report, weighted by how much. Two or more donors holding 25% of the money freeze a case. Fake accounts have no say.

🛡

Loan guarantee

A missed repayment can be declared by anyone. The guarantee pays lenders first and the borrower is blocked from new loans.

⚖

Verifiers have skin in the game

Every verifier locks 5% of a grant. If fraud is confirmed, the stake is paid to the donors on top of their refund, and the verifier is removed.

🏛

Approved hospitals

Hospitals apply with a licence fingerprint, the owner approves them, and a different verifier must approve every hospital campaign.

📜

Public audit trail

Every donation, release, refund and report is written by the contract into a list anyone can read. Nobody edits it.

Two ways to help

Grants for urgent care. Loans for livelihoods.

Grant

A gift, no repayment

For treatment, surgery, dialysis, rehabilitation and emergencies.

  • Paid straight to the provider
  • Released in milestones
  • Refunded if the goal is missed
Loan

Repaid in parts

For a farm pump, a sewing machine, a small shop. No interest.

  • Verifier locks a 20% guarantee
  • Lenders claim as repayments arrive
  • Default blocks new loans

Honest limits

This is a prototype on NEAR testnet: no real money and no real hospitals. A verifier can still be fooled, and no contract can force a borrower to repay. These safeguards reduce the damage, they do not remove the risk. Real use needs legal review and identity checks.
